Connection

not connected

Your wallet asks you to approve VeilNet, and asks again for every payment. VeilNet never sees your seed or your keys. This is the safer choice.

Open your DERO wallet, turn on XSWD, then press Connect. A prompt appears in the wallet.

VeilNet talks to a local wallet JSON-RPC server. Fill in the user and password only if you started the wallet with --rpc-login.

Keep the endpoint on 127.0.0.1. VeilNet never sends these credentials anywhere else.

Run without a wallet. Demo nodes and free nodes still work; paid nodes will not connect.

Connecting only reads your address and balance. No transfer happens until you approve one.

Traffic shaping

Pad hides how big your packets are. Constant rate also hides when you send them, as long as your link keeps up — it costs real bandwidth. Neither hides you from someone who can watch both ends of the internet.

Entry transport: direct (plain UDP). No censorship-resistant transport ships in this build.
